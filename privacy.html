Privacy Policy

Cold Hours Uploader · Last updated 23 November 2026

Cold Hours Uploader ("the app") is a private, internal tool operated by the owner of the YouTube channel Cold Hours (@ColdHoursStories). It is used only by the channel owner, on the channel owner's own YouTube channel. It is not offered to the public.

What the app accesses

Through Google OAuth, the app requests the YouTube scopes youtube.upload, youtube.force-ssl and yt-analytics.readonly. It uses them only to: upload videos to the owner's channel, set thumbnails, upload caption files, update titles, descriptions and publish schedules, post comments as the channel, read basic information about the owner's own channel, and read performance statistics (views, watch time, click-through rate) for the owner's own videos so the owner can improve them.

What data is stored

The app stores an OAuth refresh token as a private environment variable so it can act on the owner's channel. It keeps no other Google user data beyond the IDs of videos the owner has uploaded. It does not access, collect or store data about any other YouTube user or viewer.

Sharing

No data is sold, shared with or transferred to third parties. The app shows no YouTube data to anyone other than the channel owner.

YouTube and Google

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you are also bound by the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. When access is revoked, the stored token stops working and is deleted.
